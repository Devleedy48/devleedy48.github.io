Applied Privacy Policy

Effective September 15, 2026

Applied is a job application tracker for iPhone made by Devon Leedy. This policy explains what information the app handles and who it's shared with.

Your job application data stays on your device

Everything you enter in Applied, including companies, roles, dates, stages, resume versions, notes, and follow-up reminders, is stored only on your device. Applied has no accounts and no servers, and the developer never receives or sees this information. Follow-up reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Advertising

Applied shows a banner ad provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect information such as:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking when the app asks
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, and app version
  • Ad interactions, such as ad views and taps, and diagnostic data

If you choose "Ask App Not to Track," the advertising identifier is not shared and you'll see ads that are less personalized. You can change this any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK, and some US states), Applied asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change that choice under Settings → Ad privacy choices in the app.

Google's use of this information is covered by Google's Privacy Policy. You can learn more at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Purchases

You can buy a one-time "Remove ads" upgrade. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; the developer never sees your payment details. Applied uses RevenueCat to confirm purchases and restore them. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history for this app. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy. After you remove ads, the ad provider is no longer contacted.

What the developer collects

The developer does not collect, sell, or share your personal information. Any data collection is limited to the third-party services described above.

Children

Applied is not directed to children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
