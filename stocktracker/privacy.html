StockTracker Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

StockTracker is an iPhone app for following stocks, practice trading with simulated money, and getting price alerts. It is made by Devon Leedy. This page explains what information the app collects and what happens to it.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, the display name you choose, and your password. Passwords are stored only as a one-way hash and can't be read by anyone.
  • What you create in the app: your watchlist, price alerts, and practice trades and balances.
  • A push notification token for your device, if you allow notifications. It's used only to deliver the price alerts you set up.
  • Purchase status: if you buy StockTracker Pro, whether your subscription or purchase is active. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; we never see your card or Apple ID details.

StockTracker does not collect your location, contacts, or photos, and it does not connect to any real brokerage account.

Ads

The free version of StockTracker shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect device information such as your IP address, device model, and how you interact with ads. iOS asks whether StockTracker may track you; only if you choose Allow does AdMob receive your device's advertising identifier and show personalized ads. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, ads are not personalized. You can change this any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. StockTracker Pro removes ads entirely.

Your watchlist, alerts, practice trades, email, and name are never shared with advertisers.

How it's used

Your account information and everything you create in the app is used only to run the app: to sign you in, save your watchlist and practice portfolios, send the alerts you ask for, and unlock Pro if you've bought it. It is never sold or rented, and it is never used for advertising. The only tracking is the optional ad tracking described above, which you control.

Who else handles data

  • Hosting: the app's server and database run on a cloud hosting provider, which stores the data above on our behalf.
  • Apple Push Notification service receives your device token and the alert text in order to deliver notifications.
  • Google AdMob serves ads in the free version, as described above.
  • RevenueCat processes in-app purchase receipts from Apple to confirm whether you have Pro. It receives an anonymous account ID and your purchase history in StockTracker, not your email or name. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.
  • Alpaca provides market data. The server requests prices for ticker symbols; none of your personal information is sent to Alpaca.

Keeping it secure

Data travels between the app and the server over encrypted HTTPS connections. Your sign-in session is stored in the iPhone's secure keychain.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time in the app under Account → Delete account. This permanently removes your account, watchlist, alerts, and practice trading history from the server right away. Deleting your account does not cancel a Pro subscription; cancel it in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. Signing out also stops alerts from being sent to that device.

Children

StockTracker is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Not financial advice

Practice trades use simulated money only. Nothing in StockTracker is investment advice, and market data may be delayed or differ from other sources.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
