Common questions

Is the money in practice trading real?

No. Every practice trade uses simulated money. StockTracker never connects to a real brokerage account and never places real orders. Nothing in the app is investment advice.

Why doesn't a price match my broker?

Prices come from the IEX exchange through Alpaca. IEX is one exchange, not the full consolidated market, so prices can differ slightly from other sources and may be delayed.

I'm not getting price alerts.

Open iPhone Settings → StockTracker → Notifications and turn on Allow Notifications. Alerts are checked about every 30 seconds. Signing out stops alerts on that device until you sign in again.

How do I restore StockTracker Pro on a new phone?

Sign in, then tap Account → Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID you bought Pro with.

How do I cancel StockTracker Pro?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Go to iPhone Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → StockTracker and cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date. Deleting the app or your account does not cancel the subscription. For refunds, use reportaproblem.apple.com.

What happens if my Pro subscription ends?

Nothing is deleted. Extra practice portfolios become read-only, existing alerts keep firing, and new items follow the free limits.

I forgot my password.

Email me from the address on your account and I'll help you get back in.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, tap Account → Delete account. Your account, watchlist, alerts and practice history are removed from the server right away.

Can I turn off personalized ads?

Yes. Go to iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking and turn StockTracker off. StockTracker Pro removes ads entirely.
