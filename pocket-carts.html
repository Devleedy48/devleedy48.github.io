Five classic games.
One pocket arcade.

Every mode is its own cartridge, but the coins, the revives and the daily challenge are shared. Play offline, on iPhone.

Download on the App Store

2048

Slide and merge tiles to reach the highest number.

Snake

Grow as long as you can without folding in on yourself.

Block Drop

Stack falling pieces and clear rows before they reach the top.

Grid Fill

Fit shapes onto the board and clear full lines.

Minesweeper

Read the numbers and flag the mines, across three difficulties.

One economy, not five apps

Coins and revives carry across every mode. Earn coins in Snake, spend them on a revive in Minesweeper. When a run ends, a revive keeps it going, and each game revives you in the way that game needs: clearing your fullest rows, rewinding a step, or flagging the mine you just hit.

A new challenge every morning

Everyone gets the same challenge each day, and the game it lands on rotates. Finish it for coins, and keep the streak alive for free revives.

Leaderboards worth chasing

Every mode has its own Game Center board, with separate Minesweeper times for Easy, Medium and Hard.

Plays anywhere

No account, no sign-up, no internet needed. Scores, coins and revives live on your device. Sound effects follow the silent switch, so the music you were already listening to keeps playing.
